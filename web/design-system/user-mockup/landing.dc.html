The card

Three lines. One fact each.

The day's change sits on top, measured from the previous close. Below it, never more than three lines — and on a quiet day, the card says so instead of filling space.

01
What happened
One fact in under 15 words, ending with its source — the company, an SEC filing, the Fed.
02
What's next
The nearest earnings date or dividend, with a countdown. Unconfirmed dates are marked (est.).
03
Price fact Plus
The most notable thing in recent closing prices — a streak, or the distance from a 52-week high.
